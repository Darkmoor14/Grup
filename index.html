SHEET 02 — ABOUT

Engineering background. Project management discipline.

Tag Stop works at the intersection of electrical engineering, mechanical systems and IT — helping companies bridge the gap between technical design and successful project delivery.

With multiple years of experience, Tag Stop has guided cross-functional teams through complex infrastructure and technology initiatives that keep modern operations running smoothly. The role centers on translating technical complexity into an actionable roadmap for the entire organization — clear scope, sound timelines, disciplined budgets, and risk that's proactively managed rather than reactively discovered.

Clients bring Tag Stop in when a project spans multiple disciplines and needs a team that understands the engineering and can run the program.
