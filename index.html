SHEET 02 — ABOUT

Engineering background. Project management discipline.

Tag Stop works at the intersection of electrical engineering, mechanical systems and IT — helping companies bridge the gap between technical design and successful project delivery.

Over the past 12 years, Tag Stop has led multidisciplinary teams through plant electrification projects, HVAC and mechanical retrofits, and the IT systems (SCADA, monitoring, ERP integrations) that keep modern facilities running. The role is to translate technical complexity into a plan the whole organization can execute against — clear scope, realistic schedule, controlled budget, and risk that's managed instead of discovered.

Clients bring Tag Stop in when a project spans multiple disciplines and needs a team that understands the engineering and can run the program.
