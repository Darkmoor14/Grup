Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This website does not use traditional third-party tracking cookies. It does use browser local storage — covered by the same consent rules as cookies under the German Telecommunications-Digital-Services-Data-Protection Act (TTDSG) and the EU ePrivacy Directive. This page explains exactly what is stored, why, and how you can control it.

What we store

NameCategoryPurposeDuration
ts_cookie_consent Necessary Remembers your cookie preference so you're not asked again on every visit. Until you clear your browser storage or withdraw consent.
theme Functional Remembers whether you chose light or dark mode. Until you clear your browser storage or switch this category off.

We do not currently use any analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking cookies. If that ever changes, this page and the consent banner will be updated first, and any new non-essential category will remain off until you actively opt in.

Legal basis

  • Necessary storage is permitted without consent under § 25(2) TTDSG, as it is strictly required to operate the site and remember your preference.
  • Functional storage is only set after you actively opt in, on the basis of your consent (§ 25(1) TTDSG, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw this consent at any time with future effect.

Managing your preferences

You can change your choice at any time using the Cookie Settings link in the footer of any page on this site. You can also clear or block local storage entirely through your browser's own privacy settings — note that doing so will also remove your saved consent choice, so you may be asked again on your next visit.
