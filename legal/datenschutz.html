3. Hosting and server log files

This website is hosted by [hosting provider, e.g. GitHub Pages / your web host]. Each time this website is accessed, the hosting provider's servers automatically record technical information ("server log files"), such as browser type and version, operating system, referring page, date and time of access, and the IP address used to make the request. This data is processed to ensure stable and secure operation of the website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR — legitimate interest in system security and stability) and is not merged with other data sources.

4. Contact form

If you use the contact form, the data you enter (name, email address, company, message, and any other details you choose to provide) is transmitted to Formspree, Inc., a third-party form-processing service, which forwards it to us by email so that we can respond to your enquiry. No copy of your submission is stored in a database on this website.

Processing is based on Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (steps taken at your request prior to entering into a contract) and, where no contract results, on Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries). Formspree may process data on servers located in the United States; where this involves a transfer of personal data outside the EU/EEA, it is carried out on the basis of the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and/or Standard Contractual Clauses. See Formspree's own privacy policy for details on their processing.

Data submitted via the contact form is retained only as long as necessary to handle your enquiry and any follow-up correspondence, after which it is deleted unless we are subject to statutory retention obligations.

5. Local storage / cookies

This website uses browser local storage rather than traditional tracking cookies. See the separate Cookie Policy for a full breakdown of what is stored, for how long, and how to manage your preferences. In summary:

  • Necessary: a record of your cookie preference itself, so we don't ask again on every visit (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Functional (only with your consent): your light/dark mode preference (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR — consent).
  • Analytics: not currently in use on this website.

6. No profiling or automated decision-making

We do not use automated decision-making, including profiling, within the meaning of Art. 22 GDPR.

7. Your rights

Subject to the conditions set out in applicable law, you have the right to:

  • request access to the personal data we hold about you (Art. 15 GDPR);
  • request rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR);
  • request erasure of your data (Art. 17 GDPR);
  • request restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR);
  • receive your data in a portable format (Art. 20 GDPR);
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest (Art. 21 GDPR); and
  • withdraw consent at any time, with future effect, where processing is based on consent (Art. 7(3) GDPR).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 1. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU/EEA member state of your habitual residence, place of work, or place of the alleged infringement.
